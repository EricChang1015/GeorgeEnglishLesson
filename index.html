Lesson 1 · Ready

George and the Little Dragon

George meets Pip, a lost little dragon. They climb the hill, find Pip’s cave home, and discover a shining red egg. Dialogue + AI voices for Narrator, George, and Pip.

Lesson 2 · Ready

The Red Egg Hatches

Continues Lesson 1: the red egg hatches into Ember, Pip’s little sister. George helps with moss and berries, and Ember flies a little. Child boy AI voice for George.

Lesson 3 · Ready

George and the Storm on the Hill

Continues Lesson 2: George picks berries with Pip and Ember, a storm and a bear appear, they stay quiet and hide, wait in the cave, then Daddy climbs up to find them. Vocab words include an example sentence + Daddy’s AI voice.
