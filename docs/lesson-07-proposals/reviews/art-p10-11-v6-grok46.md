| Footprints inland | **PASS** | Deep three-toed tracks in the sand run from the left waterline toward the throne/jungle, not back into the surf. |

| **Unfinished open green vine crown** | **FAIL** | The chair’s back rises into a built-in circular/crescent vine “halo”; there is no separate open leafy hoop hanging above or beside the seat that Beak could later lift onto the hood. |

| Beak ~2.2× (hood at waist) | **weak / borderline** | Beak’s comb-and-beak fill the middle tile while George’s hood sits at the bottom of that same tile (lower belly / waist zone). Height clears a casual ≥2× read but is not a clean locked 2.2×. |
| Goat ~2.6× (hood at mid-thigh) | **FAIL** | Goat’s ram-horn head and most of its torso live in the middle-left tile (same vertical band as Beak’s head); George’s hood is already at the bottom of the adjacent mid tile, i.e. Goat’s chest/waist, not mid-thigh — Goat reads ~1.5–2.0×, under the 2.6× lock and at risk of the ≥2× floor. |

**Page result: FAIL** — procession order, boat, empty chair, inland-pointing tracks, hood, and Horn’s height are right; the page still misses the vine bridge, the separate unfinished crown, inland-not-shore travel, and Goat’s scale.

## Summary

**Passing page:** p11.  
**Failing page:** p10.

Required redraw (p10 only, keep p11): put the locked procession **on a readable vine-and-log bridge**, leave Billy o' Tea as a **far original-shore** beat, keep the empty leaf throne, add a **separate unfinished open green vine hoop** at that chair, and restore Goat to ~2.6× with George’s hood at **mid-thigh** (Horn ~3× at the knee, Beak ~2.2× at the waist) on one ground line. Do not treat the throne’s built-in halo as the crown.

**Evidence sentence:** p11 correctly crowns the still-on hood in an ocean-free clearing with bow, smile, chair, and drum, but p10 v6 is a same-beach walk beside the moored boat — no vine bridge, no pick-up-able unfinished crown, Goat short of the mid-thigh lock — so the p10 storyboard contract is not met.

**VERDICT: FAIL**
