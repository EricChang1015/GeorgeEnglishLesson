# Lesson 7 工作分鏡（已凍結）— *George and the Wild Parade*

狀態：2026-09-01 — **家長選定 Composer 劇本**；野獸改名 Horn／Beak／Goat。GATE 1 前不出課文內頁、不產 TTS。  
來源：[`plot-composer.md`](plot-composer.md)；只改 role key、圖上外形與禁止項裡的舊「原創／勿抄造型」鎖。

英雄：**George**。服裝：全 12 頁 T-rex 連身裝。房間＝L4 RealBedroom。船＝小私人 **Billy o' Tea**。

### Page 1 — T-Rex Chase（LivingRoom）

**敘事功能：** 開場頑皮；T-rex 裝、木叉玩追 Sylvia，鬧不是傷。  
**翻頁鉤子：** 媽媽聞聲。  
**圖上必見證據：** George 棕／卡其 T-rex＋深藍運動鞋；木叉舉胸前；Sylvia 笑鬧不哭；**LivingRoom** 沙發／暖燈，無 L4 床。  
**在場：** george, sylvia  
**禁止：** 刺人、血、Sylvia 大哭、Mike／Nibble、換裝、南瓜桶。

| Role | Line | Emotion |
|------|------|---------|
| narrator | After tea, George stomped through the living room in his T-rex suit. | calm |
| george | "Roar! I am the biggest dinosaur in this whole house!" | proud |
| sylvia | "George, stop chasing me with that silly wooden fork!" | annoyed |
| george | "No way! Wild T-rex George will catch you soon!" | naughty |

### Page 2 — Wild Thing（LivingRoom）

**敘事功能：** Mummy 定調 wild thing；回嘴；禁晚餐、關房。  
**翻頁鉤子：** 被趕向房門。  
**圖上必見證據：** Mummy 門口／樓梯，嚴厲不恐怖；George 仍 T-rex、握木叉；Sylvia 在旁；指向臥室。  
**在場：** george, mummy, sylvia  
**禁止：** 恐怖懲罰、推搡、Daddy 睡前朗讀構圖。

| Role | Line | Emotion |
|------|------|---------|
| mummy | "George! You are being a wild thing, not a kind brother!" | cross |
| george | "Wild things do not have to listen to anyone!" | defiant |
| mummy | "No supper. Go to your room and stay there now." | firm |
| sylvia | "Maybe next time play without the fork, George." | gentle |

### Page 3 — Sent to Room（RealBedroom）

**敘事功能：** 禁足孤單；建立 L4 房間基線。  
**翻頁鉤子：** 牆或地板出現綠意。  
**圖上必見證據：** 米色軟墊床頭＋木邊、奶油被、淡藍床單；光鳥燈床邊；床頭左側實牆無窗；遠牆灰簾；George T-rex，木叉在床或手邊。  
**在場：** george  
**禁止：** 星形燈、床頭櫃檯燈、Mike／Nibble。

| Role | Line | Emotion |
|------|------|---------|
| narrator | George marched to his room and shut the door with a loud thump. | calm |
| george | "Fine! I do not even want any stupid supper tonight!" | sulky |
| narrator | His tummy rumbled, but nobody came to the door. | soft |
| george | "They all love Sylvia more than they love me." | lonely |

### Page 4 — Jungle Room（RealBedroom）

**敘事功能：** 叢林長出；房間錨仍可認。  
**翻頁鉤子：** 葉叢深處有小木船。  
**圖上必見證據：** 藤蔓沿奶油牆與床頭；床、光鳥燈、灰簾至少各見；床單邊緣可見；George T-rex 張望。  
**在場：** george  
**禁止：** 房間消失、pillow cave、第二個 George。

| Role | Line | Emotion |
|------|------|---------|
| narrator | Green vines crept up the cream wall beside his beige headboard. | wonder |
| george | "Wait… my bed is still here under all these leaves." | curious |
| narrator | The little bird lamp glowed warm through the curling jungle. | calm |
| george | "Even the grey curtains look like tall trees now." | amazed |

### Page 5 — Billy o' Tea（RealBedroom → Threshold）

**敘事功能：** 小私人 Billy o' Tea 現身；主動上船。  
**翻頁鉤子：** 船離開葉叢。  
**圖上必見證據：** 茶棕船身、茶色帆、茶葉圖徽、帆上無英文；一腳床沿一腳上船；無 crew／鯨。  
**在場：** george  
**禁止：** 換色換徽、Wellerman。

| Role | Line | Emotion |
|------|------|---------|
| narrator | A small wooden boat pushed through the leaves beside his bed. | wonder |
| george | "Billy o' Tea! You came just for me!" | excited |
| narrator | Tea-brown sails rose up; a leaf emblem shone soft and gold. | calm |
| george | "I will sail away and find my own wild land!" | bold |

### Page 6 — Long Sail（OpenSea）

**敘事功能：** 長航；燈光仍牽連房間。  
**翻頁鉤子：** 島影與巨大剪影。  
**圖上必見證據：** George 船尾掌舵；同一艘小船；淡紫天空；遠處或船尾一點暖光像鳥燈。  
**在場：** george  
**禁止：** 第六人、換船、換裝、鯨。

| Role | Line | Emotion |
|------|------|---------|
| narrator | George steered past fern waves under a wide lavender sky. | calm |
| george | "The sea smells like rain and something sweet." | curious |
| narrator | His room faded behind him, but the bird lamp light followed. | soft |
| george | "Keep going, Billy. I am not turning back yet." | determined |

### Page 7 — Wild Shore（WildIsland）

**敘事功能：** 抵島；三獸示威；George 不退。  
**翻頁鉤子：** 準備對視。  
**圖上必見證據：** 船停淺灘；**Horn**（牛角、條紋身、獅鬃，≈3×）、**Beak**（雞冠黃羽鳥嘴，≈2.2×）、**Goat**（捲羊角山羊鬍，≈2.6×）並排擋岸，皆 ≥2× George；黃眼、尖牙、利爪張開；George T-rex 在船頭／淺水；無武器。  
**在場：** george, horn, beak, goat  
**禁止：** 打鬥、流血、電影名、第四隻、把獸畫成和 George 差不多高。

| Role | Line | Emotion |
|------|------|---------|
| horn | "Who dares land on our island with small blue shoes?" | fierce |
| beak | "Look at those teeth on his hood! Show your claws!" | challenging |
| goat | "Three wild ones block the shore. Step back, small boy." | warning |
| george | "I am not small. I am George, and I am brave." | brave |

### Page 8 — Stare and Crown（WildClearing）

**敘事功能：** 眼神馴服；Beak 戴藤冠。  
**翻頁鉤子：** 狂歡要開始。  
**圖上必見證據：** George 直視三雙黃眼；三獸收爪退讓；Horn 先低頭；**Beak** 把葉藤冠戴上恐龍頭套；仍 T-rex；三獸仍 ≥2×。  
**在場：** george, horn, beak, goat  
**禁止：** 拳腳、武器、魔法光、冠上英文。

| Role | Line | Emotion |
|------|------|---------|
| george | "Stop. Look at my eyes. I am not afraid of you." | stern |
| narrator | The yellow-eyed beasts went quiet and lowered their sharp claws. | calm |
| horn | "You stared us down. You must be our king now." | awed |
| beak | "Wear this vine crown. Rule the wild things well." | proud |

### Page 9 — Wild Parade（WildClearing）

**敘事功能：** 跳、跺、轉圈狂歡。  
**翻頁鉤子：** George 摸肚子。  
**圖上必見證據：** 三獸＋George 同跳；藤冠在頭套上；爪牙可見但表情快樂；怪樹／藤；T-rex 尾可見。  
**在場：** george, horn, beak, goat  
**禁止：** 原句 *let the wild rumpus start*、恐怖陰影、傷害。

| Role | Line | Emotion |
|------|------|---------|
| goat | "Let the wild parade begin! Stomp and swirl with us!" | joyful |
| george | "Higher! We leap like storm clouds over the moon!" | gleeful |
| beak | "King George, your roar is the loudest of all!" | delighted |
| horn | "Again! Again! The island shakes with happy feet!" | excited |

### Page 10 — Hungry Heart（WildClearing）

**敘事功能：** 餓；冠變重；想念家。  
**翻頁鉤子：** 決定放棄王位。  
**圖上必見證據：** George 按肚子或摸冠；三獸圍近但溫柔；天光轉柔；Billy o' Tea 遠景同岸。不說這是夢。  
**在場：** george, horn, beak, goat  
**禁止：** I am dreaming、強制送回。

| Role | Line | Emotion |
|------|------|---------|
| george | "My tummy is empty. I wish I smelled hot soup." | hungry |
| narrator | The vine crown felt heavy; the jungle night felt far from home. | soft |
| goat | "Kings can leave whenever their hearts pull homeward." | gentle |
| george | "The place that loves me most is waiting for me." | tender |

### Page 11 — Homeward（OpenSea）

**敘事功能：** 自願摘冠留島；原路回航。  
**翻頁鉤子：** 遠方鳥燈暖光。  
**圖上必見證據：** George 在同一艘 Billy o' Tea；葉冠留給 **Horn** 舉著揮別；Beak、Goat 岸邊；海面暖點光。  
**在場：** george, horn, beak, goat  
**禁止：** 追船強留、換船、冠跟著回家。

| Role | Line | Emotion |
|------|------|---------|
| george | "Goodbye, wild friends. I must go back home now." | wistful |
| horn | "Sail safe, little king. We will keep your crown." | warm |
| beak | "Tell your family the wild things said hello." | friendly |
| narrator | Billy o' Tea turned toward the distant bird-lamp glow. | calm |

### Page 12 — Hot Supper（RealBedroom）

**敘事功能：** 同一 L4 房間；熱晚餐；全家四人。  
**翻頁鉤子：** （終）wild thing 被接納。  
**圖上必見證據：** 床、光鳥燈、灰簾、床頭左實牆；叢林退盡或只剩幾葉；托盤熱食蒸氣；Daddy 最高、Sylvia 高於 Mummy、George 最矮；仍整套 T-rex（**不脫頭套**）；木叉在 tray 旁或床邊；無船、無獸、無冠。  
**在場：** george, mummy, sylvia, daddy  
**禁止：** 叢林未退、缺家人、換裝、脫頭套。

| Role | Line | Emotion |
|------|------|---------|
| narrator | George stepped into his room. Supper steamed on a tray. | calm |
| mummy | "We kept your plate hot. Come and eat, wild thing." | soft |
| daddy | "Welcome home, George. We missed our brave little king." | warm |
| sylvia | "Next time chase me with hugs, not a fork!" | teasing |
