# Character reference catalog — Mummy

Likeness refs for **Mummy** (mother). Photos in this folder are **tracked in git**. Do not embed raw photos in published lesson HTML — generate picture-book art under `lessons/assets/lesson-XX/` instead.

**Family / group shots with George** remain in `lessons/assets/refs/george/` (e.g. `george-mummy-indoor-reading-smile.jpg`, `george-daddy-mummy-indoor-closeup.jpg`, `daddy-mummy-indoor-smile.jpg`). Use **this folder** for solo Mummy face, body, and outfit refs.

## Naming

```
mummy-{setting}-{pose-or-mood}[-variant].jpg
```

- Subject id: `mummy` (lowercase kebab-case; no spaces)
- When George is also in frame, file lives under `refs/george/` with `george-` prefix

## Cast lock (for story art)

| Id | Role | Appearance notes |
|----|------|------------------|
| `mummy` | Mother (~30s–40s) | East Asian adult woman; long straight dark brown/black hair (wispy bangs; sometimes low ponytail); **thin round metal glasses** (gold/silver) in most refs; oval face; warm smile. **Shorter than Sylvia**, taller than George. |

## How agents should pick refs

1. **Mummy face / likeness (required for any Mummy art):** start with `priority: primary` — `mummy-solo-indoor-smile.png` (front-facing, round glasses, wide warm smile).
2. **Gentle / calm expression:** `mummy-solo-indoor-airplane-neutral.jpg` or `mummy-solo-indoor-restaurant-neutral.png`.
3. **Outdoor / travel / rain:** `mummy-solo-outdoors-gorge-raincoat-smile.jpg`.
4. **With George (reading / bedtime):** `george-mummy-indoor-reading-smile.jpg` in `refs/george/`.
5. **With Daddy / full family:** use `lessons/assets/refs/george/CATALOG.md` group entries.
6. **Lesson-specific costume locks** (cave kit, home clothes in Lesson 7, etc.) stay in chapter bibles — do not copy casual photo clothes unless the beat needs them.
7. Read the photo with the Read tool before generating art; keep likeness consistent across a lesson.
8. Never copy a photo into a published lesson page; generate new picture-book illustrations inspired by these refs.
