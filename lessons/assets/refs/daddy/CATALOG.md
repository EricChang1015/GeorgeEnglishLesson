# Character reference catalog — Daddy

Likeness refs for **Daddy** (father). Photos in this folder are **tracked in git**. Do not embed raw photos in published lesson HTML — generate picture-book art under `lessons/assets/lesson-XX/` instead.

**Family / group shots with George** remain in `lessons/assets/refs/george/` (e.g. `george-daddy-outdoors-pavilion-neutral.jpg`, `george-daddy-bedtime-reading.jpg`, `daddy-sylvia-indoor-selfie.jpg`). Use **this folder** for solo Daddy face, body, and outfit refs.

## Naming

```
daddy-{setting}-{pose-or-mood}[-variant].jpg
```

- Subject id: `daddy` (lowercase kebab-case; no spaces)
- When George is also in frame, file lives under `refs/george/` with `george-` prefix (George listed first)

## Cast lock (for story art)

| Id | Role | Appearance notes |
|----|------|------------------|
| `daddy` | Father (~late 30s–40s) | East Asian adult man; short black hair with volume on top; **thin round/oval dark-metal glasses**; small **mole on lower left chin**; clean-shaven. **Tallest** in the family (Daddy > Sylvia > Mummy > George). |

## How agents should pick refs

1. **Daddy face / likeness (required for any Daddy art):** start with `priority: primary` — `daddy-solo-indoor-vehicle-neutral.png` (round glasses + chin mole). Strong group-shot default: `george-daddy-outdoors-pavilion-neutral.jpg` in `refs/george/`.
2. **Warm smile:** `daddy-solo-indoor-bathroom-selfie-smile.png`.
3. **Front-facing neutral (plain background):** `daddy-solo-indoor-neutral.jpg` (note: **rectangular** frames in this photo — prefer round glasses from other refs unless bible allows rectangular).
4. **Travel / outdoor:** `daddy-solo-outdoors-temple-selfie-neutral.jpg`.
5. **With George / Mummy / Sylvia / full family:** use `lessons/assets/refs/george/CATALOG.md` group entries.
6. **Lesson-specific costume locks** (life vest, cave kit, sleep shirt, etc.) stay in chapter bibles — do not copy casual photo clothes unless the beat needs them.
7. Read the photo with the Read tool before generating art; keep likeness consistent across a lesson.
8. Never copy a photo into a published lesson page; generate new picture-book illustrations inspired by these refs.
